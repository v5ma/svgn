To find out more about Facebook Horizon I interviewed Paige Dansinger, a VR artist, who was involved with creating art & experiences in the early pre-alpha version of Horizon up until the present moment.

Article by Micah Blumberg, Journalist & Software Engineer

Facebook Horizon combines the massive multiplayer online world building such as seen in games like Minecraft & Roblox, with visual game scripting seen in the popular VR app Rec Room, and the online social chat experiences of apps like AltSpace & VR Chat. All in one.

I felt that Paige was the perfect person to learn about Facebook Horizon from because she has been an early pre-alpha tester creating some of the very first Horizon worlds that you will be able to explore when Horizon launches later this month.

On Facebook Paige wrote: “In all honesty, when announced at OC6 last year I respectfully believed FB designed Horizon with me in mind… it’s like Mark Z looked right at me and said “Paige, I made this for you”.. I know they created it with everyone in mind, but inside I felt it was a personal gift. As a result of this generous (romantic fantasy) feeling I have way more dedication to the platform. Without insulting other platforms, this is the one that works best for me. I owe it so much.. it’s kept me sane and offered me escape, it’s brought me a new group of friends I feel like a family with, it’s been a lifeline offering me a schedule and purpose and a safe place for risk/failure/reward when everything irl has been so challenging this year. It’s been easy for me to look into a new horizon and create a foundation there that could be used to re-model the real world in a better way… I’ve accomplished things there I never could before from jumping off a cloud to successfully scripting.”
