I saw something on CNN back before the Election was over, this was just after the 2005 tapes of Donald Trump were released. Conway said this to CNN:

She told him after his off script rant, “You and I are in a fight for the next 17 days.” When Trump asked why, Conway replied: “Because I know you’re going to win. And that comment you just made sounds like you think you’re going to lose. And we’re going to argue about it until you win.

His response?

“He was like, ‘OK, honey. Then we’ll win,’ “ Conway said.”

In the next couple weeks Trump said “We’re Winning” several times on tv. He denied the polls were bad for him, he pushed an alternative reality in which he was winning.

Then on tv I started seeing Obama & Hillary each trying to encourage more people to get out to vote, for fear that she might lose if people didn’t go to the polls to vote for her. Obama & Hillary were expressing fear of losing, and Donald was expressing confidence in winning. Donald’s confidence was like a rally at a sports game. Obama & Hillary were depressing their own people by talking about potentially losing.

All this was trippy for me because it sounded like Conway and Trump were focused on using the Law of Attraction to win the Presidency.
