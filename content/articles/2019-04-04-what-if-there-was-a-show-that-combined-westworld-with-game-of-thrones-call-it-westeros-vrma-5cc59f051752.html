Check out this amazing intro for the new show called Westeros by artist Gilles Augustijnen and his creative team. Will we ever see Westeros turned into a real show. Perhaps not, but it should be a real show.

Software used: After Effects, C4D, Photoshop, Illustrator, Substance Painter, ZBrush, Fusion360, DAZ3D.

Visit westeros.world for a breakdown and additional information.

Westeros World
A project that came to fruition over the course of 8 months of on and off work during spare time and countless…www.westeros.world

Westeros by artist Gilles Augustijnen is “a homage to the two iconic drama series Game of Thrones and Westworld and to title designers Patrick Clair and Raoul Marks.

“The project came to fruition over the course of 8 months of on and off work on it in the artists spare time. “

“The idea was sparked by the beautiful mashup by composer Brandon Chapman of the themes of the Game of Thrones and Westworld series.”

“WesterosWorld was also a way for these artists to achieve the level of quality of the original title sequence.”
