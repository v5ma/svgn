# Privacy in the age of Internet of Things, Alexa, Google Assistant, with Homomorphic Encryption and…

*At GTC 2018 I interviewed Nigel Cannings with Intelligent Voice*

![](/wiki/svgn/articles/media/svgn-88472105/000.jpg)

by Micah Blumberg, Researcher and Journalist: About

Intelligent Voice can make your personal data secure, such as voice data collected by Amazon Echo, Google Home, Apple Speaker, secure from even cloud providers like previously mentioned companies because their solution, which is based on an implementation of homomorphic encryption combined with artificial neural networks allows companies to run computational processes on your data without ever decrypting your data in the cloud.
