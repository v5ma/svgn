# Apple leak: The rumored iPhone XX Pro (mockup provided by SVGN.io)

*by Micah Blumberg, April 1st 2018*

![](/wiki/svgn/articles/media/svgn-88472090/000.jpg)

by Micah Blumberg, April 1st 2018

Iphone XX Pro has two fullsize screens on each side of the phone + two sets of depth cameras on each side so it can do depth tracking of two people in a conversation and capture a depth map of an entire room, creating a hologram of your meeting that you can broadcast live.

![](/wiki/svgn/articles/media/svgn-88472090/001.jpg)

The Phone is meant to sit on a mini-monopod during holographic captures of conversations and it will be considered as a device that is meant for Pro users since the MSRP is expected to be $2100 for the base model which has only 500 GB.

![](/wiki/svgn/articles/media/svgn-88472090/003.jpg)

Happy April fools day!
