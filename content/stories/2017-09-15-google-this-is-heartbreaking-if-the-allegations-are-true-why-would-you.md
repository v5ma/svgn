This morning I noticed the guardian’s article about a lawsuit that is going to become a class action lawsuit if the people behind this lawsuit have their way.

A big tech company should be able to show the truth to all of us with data. Use your cameras to create a Volumetric Video of all your offices, quantify all work done by all genders, using every real world item as an internet of things. Track every piece of paper in the building, every hand written note, it should be part of a digital hologram of your offices, so that every moment inside the company is preserved a point cloud that an executive could drop into via the HTC Vive with some software that features geometric neural networks to quantify spatial concepts, and to relate those spacial concepts to concepts learned in traditional 2D computer vision, and to sounds, voices, words, songs etc…

You should be able to let the facts speak for themselves, and you should be able to be honest about it. Hiding the truth just makes the problem worse so please resist the urge to lie and the urge to do a cover up and instead let the evidence speak for itself.
