# I didn’t think I would be able to persuade Mark Zuckerberg, with a letter, to let me be the next…

*Here is why I am saying this and what I would do differently from what Mark Zuckerberg has been doing in the past.*

I said that I wanted to be the CEO of @Meta because 1. Based on his comments & actions this year it seems to me like Mark Zuckerberg might be losing enthusiasm for his job. In his own words his company might be heading towards decline, and his 2nd officer, Sheryl Sandberg, Meta’s former COO recently jumped ship to focus on philanthropy full time.

I see potential for the company to reverse its decline dramatically by changing its direction now.

I think of Democratic Governments as representative of large numbers of people. What Ocasio-Cortez & Warren want is representative of what lots of Meta’s customers actually want.

As a corporation, at the root level, Meta has the job of generating revenue for its shareholders. That’s why it continues to exist. To accomplish this job Meta needs to pay attention to what its customers want, and then figure out a way to monetize the services that it is providing to those customers.

1. Meta needs to understand what the Governments that represent the people want, because those are Meta’s real customers.

2. Meta needs to figure out a way to make money by giving the people what they want.

3. If Ocasio-Cortez & Warren want higher standards for what is allowed to be an advertisement, then Meta needs to raise their standards for what kinds of ads are allowed, and Meta needs to charge more money to provide this more costly service.

4. If the Government wants to break up a monopoly because it is unresponsive to the public’s needs, then let’s have Meta figure out a path to making that break up profitable. I propose a social network franchise model. Meta could spin off copies of Instagram, Facebook, Whatapp, to Governments, Corporations, and Individuals. License those copies, and make them interoperable with Facebook, Whatsapp, and Instagram. Essentially allowing other entities to become owner operators of a social network that can earn revenue through Meta’s ad partnerships & links to Meta’s networks.

Meta could split off instances of itself, copies, license them to Governments & Corporations, and generate revenue through ad partnerships, and through a contract that keeps these instances connected to Meta’s ecosystem in a way that does not compromise user privacy or permit Oligarchies & Fascists to have a predatory relationship over the poor masses.

Mark Zuckerberg apparently loves McDonalds, so maybe the franchise business model can work for social networks? What if McDonald’s was able to purchase its own instance of Instagram, Facebook, and Whatsapp that was interoperable with Meta’s software without transferring all the private information that Meta has collected over time to McDonalds? It’s a thought experiment. I’m not happy with McDonald’s menu personally so I don’t eat there.

I do not believe that social networks are corrosive to Democracy. Twitter’s use of artificial intelligence to translate Ukrainian, Russian, German, English and many other languages is an understated example of how social networks with artificial intelligence are transforming global politics and helping to end genocide.

In my vision Social Networks & Artificial Intelligence can help billions of people, they can become trustworthy tools that are pro-Democracy, and anti-fascist.

On Twitter I’ve said a lot about WebXR, particularly about React Three Fiber, but also Aframe, I think that there is a path to the Metaverse that has been overlooked, and I think that the existing WebXR technologies, such as Exokit Web provide a path to bring Meta’s existing social networks into the Metaverse.

Exokit Web allows multiple WebXR webpages to be co-rendered into the same scene. This is exactly what we need to start the WebXR Metaverse, because that first canvas can be the home canvas that contains & keeps you, your avatar, your items, your digital objects, and the next canvas is the webpage that you visit. That’s the metaverse of the Oasis, now you combine this with the beautiful graphics that you see in React Three Fiber, and bam I’ve defined in one paragraph a plausible path for the future of the Metaverse with WebXR (I’ve been thinking about this for over a year.)

The CEO’s job is to recognize & communicate at a high level what needs to be done for the company to prosper, and for their customers to benefit from the interaction.

I would help chart a path to the real Metaverse, that is based on prioritizing open web standards, like WebXR. I would work with researchers to create VR & AR social network enabled research tools that rapidly accelerate innovation in various industries especially in the areas of medical research.
