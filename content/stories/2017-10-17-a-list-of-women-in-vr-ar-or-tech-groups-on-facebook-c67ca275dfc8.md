# A list of Women in VR, AR, or Tech groups on Facebook

*by Micah Blumberg, Silicon Valley Global News*

![](/wiki/svgn/articles/media/svgn-88471856/000.jpg)

by Micah Blumberg, Silicon Valley Global News

There are a lot of Women in VR, XR, MR, Tech groups on Facebook. Why not join them all? Which ones are your favorites? If I am missing any big ones that have like over a thousand members let me know I’m happy to add them to the list. There is no particular order except that the VR related groups are first.
