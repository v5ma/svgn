# Arianna Huffington will be leading the inspirational keynote at 10am on Thursday at the Samsung…

*by Micah Blumberg, Silicon Valley Global News & The Neural Lace Podcast*

by Micah Blumberg, Silicon Valley Global News & The Neural Lace Podcast

Arianna Huffington is the Founder and CEO of Thrive Global VR and AI sessions at SDC Gear VR and Gear 360 fans get ready for news from SDC 2017! AI Developers there are some great sessions here for you too, scroll down for my session picks.

### What’s on The Horizon: A Look at the Future of VR at Samsung

At 1 pm Wednesday get to Session Room #2010

Since Samsung introduced Gear VR in 2014, there have been many innovations in virtual reality. In this session, we will share what’s on the horizon for Samsung in the VR space and offer a glimpse into where we are headed across devices, software and services. Taeyong Kim, VP, Samsung

### How to be a Rock Star VR Java Developer!

AI (Artificial Intelligence) Session Highlights:

### Bixby: Our Goal and Challenge

At 1:00 pm on Wed Oct 18 get to Room #2006

“Samsung’s new intelligent agent, Bixby, was launched this year and is now available in more than 200 markets. In this session, we will introduce you the vision of Bixby, lessons learned so far, and a couple of future milestones of Bixby. We will also introduce the other Bixby-related sessions we prepared. This will be a good overview session if you are interested in Bixby.”

Brad Park, Vice President, Big Data R&D, Mobile Communications Business, Samsung.

### It Takes a Village to Raise… Bixby!

At 2pm (Wed Oct 18) stay in Session Room #2006

“The old saying that “it takes a village to raise a child” proves to be true when developing Bixby to become a truly intelligent assistant. This session will focus on Bixby Voice, where participants will be able to understand how Bixby learns and adapts to its user, while users customize commands with words of preference. You will also get a chance to get updated on our plans on building it as a open yet personalized intelligence platform. We will show you the importance of communal effort not only when it comes to raising a human child but also when enriching your bright sidekick.”

Euntaek Lim, Director, Samsung

2pm Wednesday, Disruptor Theater:

“Hear tips and tricks by expert game developers in developing and optimizing games using new technologies including Vulkan, SPIR-V, and ASTC.”

Daemyung Jang, Engineer, Graphics R&D, Samsung  Jungwoo Kim, Principal Engineer, Graphics R&D, Samsung

### Towards an AI Driven Open Metaverse

4pm, Wednesday, Session Room #2010

“We are entering an era in which digital services and communication are going to be rendered spatially. This is the true promise of AR/VR/Mixed reality content, a metaverse where you can create and share and information with true presence. Everything from devices to the web to the social fabric of society will be affected enormously by the decisions that we make today. Ready or not, the tools of tomorrow are already being adopted to build an AI driven open metaverse.”

Will Mason, Co-Founder and President, Upload  Nicolas Meuleau, Director of AI Research, Unity Technologies  Jules Urbach, CEO, OTOY Inc.

### Samsung Internet VR: Bringing Virtual Reality to the Web

“Excited about web based virtual and augmented reality? This talk will feature invited experts from various sectors of the immersive web community that will introduce frameworks for creating WebVR/AR experiences, showcase content partners using the technology today, cover the evolution of Samsung Internet on GearVR devices, and take a look at upcoming trends defining the future of this medium.”

Alban Donoyel, Co-Founder & CEO, Sketchfab  Laszlo Gombos, Senior Director, Samsung  Damon Hernandez, Product Manager and Developer Advocate, Samsung

### Thursday Session Highlights:

After the Inspirational Keynote get to the Disrupter Theater

### Limbic VITALICS: Core -and Neuro- Rehabilitation Using Gear VR

12:30 pm Disruptor Theater (Thu Oct 19)

“Limbic VITALICS, an open collaboration project using Samsung VR technology and the Limbic Chair, is used for neuro and muscular rehabilitation. The Limbic Chair, developed by a former MIT neuroscience and medicine researcher, works harmoniously with Samsung’s Gear VR to create an extremely immersive experience that simulates various parts of the brain.”

Dr. Patrik Künzler, CEO, Limbic Life

### ARCore: Augmented Reality at Android Scale

1:00 pm (Thu Oct 19) Session Room #2010

“ARCore ​​is a brand new platform from Google that allows developers to build augmented reality (AR) experiences at Android scale. This session will provide developers with everything they need to start creating the next generation of smartphone AR apps, including a deep dive into the fundamental technologies that enable ARCore, an overview of the ARCore SDKs for Java, Unity, and Unreal, and a selection of technical and design best practices.”

Nathan Martz, Product Manager, Google

Stan Lee, Marvel Comics  Rain Paris  Rob Prior, Artist, Director, Producer, Creator
