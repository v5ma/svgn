TriPointLabs : Demo

Our cloud-based platform provides the means to close the learning gap. We provide fully immersive VR/AR teaching realms that allow instructors to lead a group of students on-site or remotely (regardless of device — phone/tablet/web/VR/AR).

Home | TriPointLab Edit description www.tripointlab.com

Students using a tablet in India, for example, can follow along with a Stanford professor who may be demonstrating a lesson in VR. No competitors accommodate devices in this way.

kunze Edit description www.kevinkunze.com

### Virtual Bytes

Virtual Bytes VR demo’s focus on hacking the brain body connection using ideas from the latest cognitive neuroscience research.

Virtual Bytes is creating Virtual Reality Demos | Patreon Become a patron of Virtual Bytes today: Read 16 posts by Virtual Bytes and get access to exclusive content and… www.patreon.com

Virtual Bytes will be showing:

Play Me Back — an app that allows you to Record your shadow boxing and fight yourself, record a choreography and teach a friend or give yourself an actual hug in VR!

Look At Me — an experience where hundreds of eyes follow your every move which can be used for exposure therapy for social anxiety

Burn It — An anger management VR app.

Duet — Expand your sense of self by connecting to the body and movements of a professional ballerina.

“The power of VR over brains — Using ideas from predictive coding, the latest cognitive neuroscience framework , we will explain the unique power VR has over brains and showcase some examples of how this power is being used in academic research.”

### NeuroExplorerVR experience directed by Fifer Garbesi

Synthetic Reality in the Age of Neuroscience: It’s All in Your Head

Evolving quickly alongside the realities industries is our understanding of neuroscience. Let’s examine the current landscape of brain machine interfaces and the possibilities for thecontinuing evolution of humankind in the future!

### NeuroExplorer VR (Early Alpha Demo)

Experience the evolution of the human brain from brainstem to neocortex and stimulate brain function with a virtual brain machine interface, the Neuron Wand. This virtual reality experience is an educational tool for anyone to begin learning about the complex, but increasingly important field of neuroscience in this critical phase of humanities evolution as we begin exploring brain machine interfaces.

Produced by Fifer Garbesi, Developed by Troy Allen Norcross, Art by Micah Zayner

Runtime: 5 Minutes

### About: Fifer Garbesi

Fifer Garbesi aims to unify and create conversation through immersive technology. Her most recent experience, ¡Viva la Evolución!, explores globalization through the lens of electronic dance music in Cuba. It screened at Cannes Film Festival, The World VR Forum, and Raindance. Co-founder of ARTandVR, she seeks to build community between the art and technology worlds.

### Jay Truesdale — Demo

Jay will present the final project for his Cinematography and Advanced Editing Classes. His class is required to attend and show their work. Each student will bring a mobile device and viewer, fully charged and preloaded with their section of the project. Jay told his class that they will have to interact with actual humans to show off and explain their work. The pressure has been great. It’s really forged the classes.
