Self-Contradictory Positioning & Rhetoric The level of corruption and malice in Russian President Vladimir Putin’s actions justifies intense criticism and even demands for his total accountability. His statements claiming the West is attempting to control Russia are merely a smokescreen: Russia, under his leadership, has orchestrated horrific acts abroad, committing horrifying atrocities & crimes against humanity, and has thus forfeited its entitlement to respect on the global stage. Putin’s portrayal of the West as a threat to Russian sovereignty is misleading; in reality, the West opposes Russia’s aggressive expansionism, aiming only to restrict Russia’s incursions beyond its borders.

Putin's actions and rhetoric reveal deep contradictions that are proving destructive not only to Russia but to global stability. He consistently claims that the West is trying to "put Russia in its place," yet this narrative deliberately misrepresents reality. The West's actual position is simply to maintain Russia within its legitimate borders and prevent its invasions of neighboring countries. Putin twists this defensive stance into a false narrative about threats to Russian sovereignty.

By persistently framing the West as an aggressor, Putin has alienated Russia from many international allies and potential partners who view his rhetoric with suspicion. This portrayal of Russia as a 'fortress under siege' not only strains diplomatic relations with Western countries but also complicates its interactions with other states, including some within BRICS. These nations, while aligned with Russia on certain issues, are wary of being associated with its increasingly unpredictable foreign policy. As Putin doubles down on this narrative, he risks isolating Russia not only from Western markets but from a broader global network that sees stability and cooperation as the path to mutual prosperity. This dynamic underscores the self-defeating nature of Putin’s strategy, as his appeals to sovereignty and independence paradoxically weaken Russia's international standing.

At the 2024 BRICS summit in Kazan, Russia, Putin sought to present the bloc as a counterbalance to Western influence, using it to strengthen Russia’s global position amid its ongoing war in Ukraine. However, the summit was shadowed by mixed reactions from BRICS members, highlighting the limits of support for Russia's stance. Some members, including the UAE and Egypt, have openly opposed Russia’s invasion of Ukraine at the United Nations. China, while a strong ally, has taken a cautious approach, balancing economic support for Russia with calls for peace, which adds pressure for diplomatic resolution rather than continued conflict​

Notably, Brazilian President Lula did not attend in person, opting to participate remotely due to a health issue, which some analysts interpreted as signaling Brazil’s ambivalence toward Russia’s wartime actions. Moreover, several new BRICS members, including nations from the Global South, voiced concerns over the war's impact on regional stability. United Nations Secretary-General Antonio Guterres, present at the summit, called for an end to conflicts like Ukraine, reflecting the broader sentiment among some BRICS participants for diplomatic rather than military solutions​

Putin's claim about preventing Russia from becoming a "vassal state" appears to have backfired - the isolation from Western markets and technology has increased Russia's economic dependence on China, creating the very situation he claimed to be preventing.
